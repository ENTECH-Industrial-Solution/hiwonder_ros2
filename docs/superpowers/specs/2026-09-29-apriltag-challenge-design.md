# AprilTag Tracking challenge ("walk to tag 2 and stop where the arm can reach")

Date: 2026-09-29
Status: implemented; values measured in the sim
Chosen by: Claude, on the user's instruction to pick the remaining workshop exercises overnight;
the user reviews it in the morning. Same pattern as the other exercises.

## Purpose

Workshop topic 6.8 "AprilTag Tag Tracking" has 10 minutes. The sim's `apriltag_track` demo
(upstream's example: a yaw PID keeps the tag centred, a distance PID holds `stop_distance`)
follows one tag id. Participants fix three of four values so the robot walks to the right tag, stops where
the arm could pick from its pedestal, and does it in time. One check takes about 30 s.

What they should take away: the tag id is how the robot tells targets apart, the standoff is a
camera-to-tag distance, and the speed cap bounds the approach time.

## Scene: `worlds/apriltag_challenge.sdf`

The colour exercise's bare floor and sun, and three tag stations (pedestal, post, tag board;
`stations.station_sdf`) facing the spawn point: tag 0 at (1.0, 0.45), tag 1 at (1.2, 0.0),
tag 2 at (1.0, -0.45). Generated by `tools/make_apriltag_world.py` from
`rospider_gazebo/tag_check.py`'s `STATIONS`.

## Tracker change

`apriltag_track.py` gained `start` (default true, as before) and a `~/set_running` SetBool
service; with `start: false` it holds still until switched on, so the checker sees the walk from
the first step. (The flag is `following`: `VisionDemo` already uses `running` for its main loop.)

## The participant's file: `config/apriltag_challenge.yaml`

`/**: ros__parameters:` with the tracker's four values, validated by `challenge_params.merge` and
passed by `apriltag_challenge.launch.py` to `apriltag_track.launch.py` (with `start: false`).
The requirement is written at the top of the file: walk to tag 2 and stop where the arm can reach
(camera about 0.30-0.40 m from the tag) within 40 s.

| Key | Starting | Solved | Measured symptom |
|---|---|---|---|
| `target_tag` | 1 | 2 | the robot takes a 0.1 m step toward tag 1 and stops (level 1) |
| `stop_distance` | 0.8 | 0.35 (0.30-0.40 pass) | stops 0.94 m from the board (level 2) |
| `speed_limit` | 0.01 | 0.05 | reaches the right spot after 73 s (level 3) |
| `turn_limit` | 0.2 | (right) | — |

## The checker: `check_tag.py` / `rospider_gazebo/tag_check.py`

Refuses unless the robot is within 0.1 m of the spawn; switches the tracker on (resent if the
reply is lost); feeds odometry to `TagRun` until the robot has moved more than 5 cm and then stood
still (less than 5 mm in 5 s), never moved in 20 s, or 120 s passed; switches it off. It gives up
after 10 wall seconds without `/odom`.

| Level | Passes when |
|---|---|
| 1 To tag 2 | the board the robot ends up facing is tag 2's |
| 2 The right distance | stopped 0.40-0.55 m from tag 2's board (base to board; `stop_distance` 0.30-0.40 measured 0.42-0.53) and facing it within 15 degrees |
| 3 In time | stopped within 40 s (answer key 14-16 s) |

Hints name what the robot did (didn't move / stopped near tag N / stopped X m away / slow), never
a key.

## As built: fixes from the final review (2026-09-29)

- Level 1 judges the board the robot faces, not the nearest one: the yaw loop keeps the followed
  tag centred, so the right tag with a far or centimetre standoff (a short or backward walk) is a
  level-2 distance problem, not "wrong tag". (The starting file's 0.1 m step ended nearer tag 0's
  board while facing tag 1.)
- A run still walking when time is up, or turned to tag 2 without moving, gets a speed hint.
- The level-1 hint no longer blames occlusion (the stations cannot block tag 1 from the spawn).
- Both checkers switch the robot off when every start request went unanswered.

Deferred minors: a lost first start reply starts the tracker's clock late; stillness ignores
turning in place; a very slow sim is reported as lost odometry; `gui` leaks to the tracker as an
unused parameter.
